        ⭐ Star Scoop

        Move with the left and right arrow keys or A and D. On a phone, drag across the play area to steer.

        Catch bright stars for points and avoid space junk. You have two lives; your best score stays in this browser.

        🌙 Moonfall: Lost Signal

        Collect all three crystals, jump the spike fields, and wait for pulsing gates to open. Save pulse charges for a dangerous crossing.

        Desktop: arrows or A/D to move, W/Space to jump, X/Shift to pulse. Phone: hold MOVE and tap JUMP or PULSE.

        🏎️ Rift Rally: Wildfront

        Explore the canyon, collect seven scrap caches, blast raider drones, then reach the gold Rift Gate. Avoid the rocks and protect your hull.

        Desktop: WASD/arrows to drive, Space to fire, Shift to boost. Phone: steer with the pad, tap FIRE and BOOST.

        Friend races and game data. Host a Rift Rally room, share its temporary room code with a friend, and keep both game pages open. The games save best scores/times in this browser. Friend mode uses PeerJS to help browsers connect and exchanges gameplay positions and shots; there is no player account or chat. Only share a room code with someone you trust, since direct browser connections can reveal network details to the other player.
